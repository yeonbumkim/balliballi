Different buildings need different approaches

The same clog needs completely different equipment — and different conversations — depending on whether it's your unit's pipe or the building's shared line.

Apartments · Villas · Officetels

We distinguish your unit's traps and flex-pipes from the building's shared riser and main. If several units have trouble at once, unit-level work won't fix it — we identify the section on camera, then coordinate with your building management office (gwalliso) for you, in Korean.

Restaurants · Cafés · Retail

Kitchen lines almost always clog from congealed grease, so high-pressure jetting is essential. We can work before opening hours or overnight, and set up a weekly-to-monthly preventive jetting schedule that costs far less than emergency call-outs.

Houses · Townhouses (Hannam, Itaewon, UN Village)

We check not just indoor drains but yard manholes and septic connections. Winter freezing, leaf and soil intrusion, and sunken old clay pipes are common — we base any replacement recommendation on actual camera footage you can keep.

Frequently asked questions

Does the plumber speak English?

Your entire conversation with us — quotes, scheduling, updates — happens in English. The plumber we send is a local Korean professional and may not speak English, but you won't need it: we explain the diagnosis and price in your chat, and interpret by phone if anything comes up on site.

How much will it cost?

Standard clogs run ₩70,000–120,000; deep blockages needing high-pressure jetting run ₩120,000–200,000; building main lines start from ₩150,000 with an on-site quote. The price is always confirmed with you before work starts — and if we can't fix it, it's ₩0.

Should my landlord pay, or me?

In Korea, clogs from daily use (hair, wipes, food waste) are usually the tenant's responsibility; aging pipes, structural defects and in-wall leaks are the landlord's. We give you a written diagnosis with camera footage that you can forward to your landlord or real-estate agent (budongsan) — it usually settles the question quickly.

How fast can you get here?

As fast as possible — once details and price are confirmed, we dispatch the nearest available plumber and text you a live ETA. Gangnam, Yongsan and Seocho are our core dispatch zones. No night or weekend surcharge.

Can I pay by card? Will I get a receipt?

Yes — Korean and international credit cards, cash, or bank transfer. We issue official receipts and card slips you can submit to your landlord, employer, or insurance.

I already poured chemical drain cleaner in. Is that a problem?

Please tell us before we arrive — chemical residue can be hazardous to the technician. We follow a separate procedure: safely flushing the residue first, then finishing with high-pressure jetting.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. If the same blockage returns within 1 year of the service date, we re-do the work free. You'll get a written warranty card with your service record, plus prevention tips in English.
