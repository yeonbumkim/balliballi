How we were paid

No provider ever paid us.

While the booking service ran, Balli Balli took no commission, no referral cut and no advertising money from any plumber, cleaner, locksmith or tutor we put in front of a customer. Whichever one you picked, we earned exactly the same. That was the whole point — when we told you which quote to take, there was nothing in it for us except being right.

One disclosure that still applies: a few of our guides carry shopping links to retailers such as Coupang, and those earn us a small commission. They are marked as such on the page. It changes nothing above — no plumber, cleaner, locksmith, mover or tutor ever paid us — and a link to a shop was never the reason we recommended anything.
