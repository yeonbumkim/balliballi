💡 Free tip: don't dump furniture on the street

Leaving a sofa or mattress by the curb without a district disposal sticker can lead to a fine — buildings report it, and there are cameras. Stickers cost roughly ₩2,000–15,000 per item depending on size and district. We buy the right sticker, carry the item down, and place it at the designated spot — or donate it if it's still in good shape.

Frequently asked questions

How do small moves work in Korea?

For studios and one-rooms, Koreans book a 용달 (yongdal) — a small truck with a driver, plus helpers if needed. It's cheap and fast, but booking runs on Korean phone calls. Tell us your two addresses, floors, elevator situation and photos of your stuff, and we arrange the right truck and crew at a fixed price.

What's a ladder truck, and do I need one?

Many Korean apartment elevators can't fit large furniture, so movers use a 사다리차 (ladder truck) that lifts items through your window or balcony — a completely normal sight here. It needs building-management approval and sometimes a road arrangement; we coordinate all of that in Korean.

Can I just leave old furniture on the street?

No — dumping without a district disposal sticker (대형폐기물 스티커) can mean a fine. Every large item needs its own paid sticker and a designated drop spot. We buy the sticker, haul the item down, and place it correctly — or donate items that are still usable.

Can you assemble furniture I bought online?

Yes — IKEA, Coupang, 오늘의집 orders, anything flat-packed. We assemble, wall-mount where needed (with your landlord's OK on drilling), and take away the packaging, which Korea's recycling system is picky about.

Can you help on move-out day with my landlord?

Yes — the move-out inspection, deposit handover and utility final readings all happen on the same day, in Korean. Add our interpretation service and we'll be on the phone (or on site) to make sure nothing gets lost in translation, especially around deposit deductions.

Can I pay by card? Will I get a receipt?

Yes — Korean and international credit cards, cash, or bank transfer. Official receipts issued.
