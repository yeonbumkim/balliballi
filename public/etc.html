Frequently asked questions

What counts as "anything else"?

If it's a real-life problem in Korea that needs a local to solve it, it probably counts: a broken AC, cockroaches, setting up internet, a phone call you can't make in Korean, mounting a TV, a bill you don't understand. Message us — the first answer is free, and if it's not something we can do, we'll point you to someone who can.

How does pricing work for one-off requests?

We scope your request, find the right professional, and send one fixed quote in the chat — their price plus our coordination fee, listed separately. Simple call-making and translation tasks start from ₩20,000. You approve the total before anything is booked.

Can you just make a phone call for me in Korean?

Yes — one of our most-used services. Delivery disputes, clinic appointments, telecom cancellations, landlord conversations. Tell us what you need said and what outcome you want; we make the call and report back in English, usually the same day.

I'm a traveler, not a resident. Can I still use this?

Absolutely. Lost luggage follow-ups, Airbnb issues, pharmacy guidance, Korean-only phone reservations — travelers are half the reason this service exists. All you need is WhatsApp.

What can't you do?

We don't provide legal, medical, immigration or visa advice — for those we can only help you find and book the right licensed professional. We can't help with anything unlawful. And safety emergencies should always go to 112 (police) or 119 (fire/ambulance) first — both have English interpreters.

Can I pay by card? Will I get a receipt?

Yes — Korean and international credit cards, cash, or bank transfer. Official receipts issued.
