Frequently asked questions

What is a Korean move-in clean (입주청소)?

In Korea it's standard to have a professional deep clean done on an empty home before you move in — every cabinet, window frame, fan, bathroom and floor, plus removal of construction dust and previous-tenant grime. It's priced by home size and absolutely worth it. We book and supervise it for you in English.

How is cleaning priced?

Deep cleans are priced by home size: a studio typically runs ₩150,000–250,000, a 2-bedroom ₩300,000–450,000, larger apartments from ₩500,000. Regular housekeeping runs around ₩60,000 per 4-hour visit. Tell us your size (it's on your lease, in pyeong or ㎡) and we confirm a fixed quote in the chat.

Do I need to be home? Do I provide supplies?

You don't need to be home — share your door code or leave a key with the building office, and we coordinate access in Korean. Deep-clean teams bring all equipment and supplies. For regular housekeeping you can provide your preferred products or ask the cleaner to bring theirs for a small fee.

The bathroom mold keeps coming back. Can you fix that?

We remove existing mold, scrub the grout, and re-silicone where it's beyond saving. If it keeps returning, the cause is usually poor ventilation or a hidden leak — we can bring in our plumbing team to check, all in the same conversation.

Do you do Airbnb turnover cleaning?

Yes — same-day turnovers with photo confirmation: beds made, towels swapped, kitchen and bathroom reset. Recurring schedules get priority booking and a fixed per-turnover rate.

Can I pay by card? Will I get a receipt?

Yes — Korean and international credit cards, cash, or bank transfer. Official receipts issued — keep them for move-out deposit negotiations with your landlord.
